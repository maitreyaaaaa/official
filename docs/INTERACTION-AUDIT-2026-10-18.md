A check never silently edits a draft.
- Agent memory: tone approval advanced the brief revision, a fresh writer used the newly approved instruction, and internal draft approval made no external publication.
- Company Brain: graph zoom/fit/node selection, note search/empty state/clear, category filter, guide deep link, unavailable-owner simulation, backup proposal and approval, and history. A pending backup did not change coverage; approval did.
- Workspace chat: a graph-backed answer cited notes, and a citation opened its note. Chat starts closed.
- Plugins: all 25 connect actions and all 25 disconnect actions completed. Connected-only filtering showed 25 then the empty state at zero. Search, details, empty-state recovery, company isolation, and browser persistence were checked.

## Bugs found and fixed

1. An approved edit of a source draft changed a dependent email's finding through `REUSES_DRAFT`, even though the email text stayed broad. Approval now materializes the old promise on dependent drafts and removes their reuse link before replacing the selected draft's promise. Live FalkorDB regression: after the landing-page edit, landing became Supported and the unchanged email remained Contradicted (1 Contradicted, 2 Supported, 1 Not checked).
2. Reset previously restored rollout, test, tone, and backup but left approved draft edits and promise links in place. Reset now restores the selected company's canonical draft records and graph links. Live regression returned Northstar to 2 Contradicted, 1 Supported, 1 Not checked.
3. A Northstar unlinked draft used CSV-specific fallback wording. The checker now says “No reviewed product promise is linked to this draft.”
4. Launch review used “1 drafts”; its heading now handles zero, one, and many correctly. The draft-filter tab also announces a singular draft correctly.
5. Plugin Help previously described campaign checks; it now explains browser-local demo connections. The company header uses the selected company's initial, and a decorative fake menu ellipsis was removed.

## Limits

The 89 deterministic tests and passing production build cover data rules, route guards, and known transitions. Browser work exercised action families and all plugin connection instances, not every possible permutation of network failure and historical state. The optional OpenAI assistant is unavailable without a configured key. Plugins are browser-local demos, and real provider authentication or ingestion is not implemented. The app uses fictional graph data and does not authenticate reviewers or publish content.
