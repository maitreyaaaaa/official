Meet official, a workspace for manual underwriting of launch claims. A reviewer checks each claim against product, rollout, and audience facts. official links each promise to reviewed product, rollout, test, and audience records. Reviewers follow each source. Each claim gets one result: Supported, Contradicted, Unknown, or Not checked. Missing or old evidence cannot prove a claim. Milo guides the fictional workspace tour. Open a finding to inspect its sources. If copy needs a change, official prepares an internal proposal. It does not publish. People approve each change. The writing handoff carries approved guidance. The FalkorDB graph links claims to their evidence. Plugins preview connections; they do not import data.
